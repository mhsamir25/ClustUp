## Key Discussion Points & Decisions

### 1. Project Management & Tracking
* All tasks, features, and milestones will be organized and tracked through **GitHub Issues** and a **GitHub Kanban Project Board** to maintain clear accountability and visibility.

### 2. Version Control & Branching Policy
* All development work will be carried out on dedicated **feature branches**.
* Changes may only be merged into the `main` branch once they have been thoroughly tested and verified to be stable.

### 3. Tech Stack & Platform Approval
* Presented the updated architecture to Sir and received approval to:
  * Migrate the core implementation stack to **Rust**.
  * Build ClustUp as a **native desktop application** instead of a web application.

### 4. Incremental Development Strategy
* Because ClustUp is an experimental systems project with a steep learning curve across multiple domains, Sir advised taking an **incremental approach**: build small, focused milestones one at a time, ensuring every deliverable is stable and usable before expanding scope.
